# Chain Research Schema

Each chain will eventually have a canonical record under chains/<chain-slug>/.

Recommended files:
- PROFILE.md
- RPC.md
- DEX_UNIVERSE.md
- FLASH_LIQUIDITY.md
- LENDING_LIQUIDATION.md
- ORDERFLOW_MEV.md
- INTENTS_RFQ.md
- CROSS_CHAIN.md
- STRATEGIES.md
- OPPORTUNITY_NOTES.md
- VALIDATION.md

Each record must separate:
1. Verified current facts.
2. Historical facts.
3. Assumptions.
4. Unknowns.
5. Research gaps.
6. Evidence.

Do not convert a research hypothesis into a project fact without validation.
