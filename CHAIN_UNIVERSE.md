# Chain Universe

## Research Rule
The project must not assume that exactly 28 chains are the permanent universe. The universe is evidence-driven and can expand beyond 28 where economically justified.

## Chain Record Requirements
For every candidate chain record:
- chain name
- chain ID
- EVM/non-EVM classification
- block time
- finality characteristics
- RPC sources
- WebSocket/event support
- DEX universe
- flash-liquidity sources
- lending/liquidation protocols
- intent/RFQ/solver surfaces
- bridge/cross-chain surfaces
- mempool/orderflow visibility
- gas model
- execution constraints
- supported scanner families
- liquidity/volume evidence
- last research timestamp
- evidence URLs
- confidence status

## Status
Full chain-by-chain verification is pending. No fabricated chain inventory is allowed.
